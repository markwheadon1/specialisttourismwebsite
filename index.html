Budget High budget

Health High blood pressure and arthritis
Hearing Bad hearing / difficulty hearing
Dental Has dentures
Food Needs soft food
Dietary Requirement Lactose intolerant
Likes Live music and TV

Customer Needs

Margaret would benefit from a comfortable cruise where she can relax without needing to walk long distances every day. Her low fitness, arthritis and use of a walking stick and occasional wheelchair mean that accessibility should be considered when choosing her ship, cabin and excursions.

Food and Accessibility

Margaret's lactose intolerance and need for soft food should be communicated to the cruise company before travelling. An accessible cabin and suitable facilities should also be requested where required. Because she has bad hearing, quieter entertainment and suitable seating would also be useful.

3 Recommended Cruises for Margaret

These are specific cruise holidays with the route included rather than simply recommending individual destinations.

Brilliance of the Seas

Royal Caribbean – 7 Night Italy & France

Barcelona → Toulon → Ajaccio, Corsica → Rome/Civitavecchia → Naples/Capri → Messina, Sicily → Sea Day → Ravenna

A Mediterranean cruise visiting several European destinations while Margaret stays onboard the same ship.

Why this suits Margaret

Margaret can unpack once while visiting several destinations. The ship gives her places to relax, restaurants and entertainment without needing to travel between hotels. Live music also matches one of her interests. She could choose shorter excursions or remain onboard when a port involves too much walking.

Brilliance of the Seas

Royal Caribbean – Italy, Spain & French Riviera

Rome/Civitavecchia → La Spezia → Florence/Pisa → Santa Margherita → Villefranche/Nice → Marseille → Sea Day → Valencia → Barcelona

A 7-night European cruise combining Italy, France and Spain.

Why this suits Margaret

This cruise includes a sea day so Margaret does not have to be active at a port every day. Her high budget could allow her to choose a more comfortable cabin and suitable facilities. She can enjoy the ship's entertainment while avoiding crowded or strenuous excursions.

Brilliance of the Seas

Royal Caribbean – Greek Isles & Turkey

Athens/Piraeus → Mykonos → Istanbul → Sea Day → Kusadasi/Ephesus → Santorini → Athens/Piraeus

A 7-night cruise visiting Greece and Turkey with time at sea.

Why this suits Margaret

Margaret can choose how much activity she wants to do at each port. The sea day gives her time to relax and enjoy the ship. Live entertainment and comfortable onboard areas suit her interests, while accessibility should be checked for individual ports before booking.
